## Robustness across selection thresholds

### Sensitivity to selection thresholds

Both groups contain well-predicted voxels. Each fraction reports the number
inside the functional LTPJ parcel divided by the total number in that group.
Direction agreement is averaged across the five cross-validation folds.

| Analysis | NC-normalized r ≥ | Direction agreement ≥ | Participants | Aligned in LTPJ, n/N (%) | Non-aligned in LTPJ, n/N (%) |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Original | 0.5 | 7/10 | Original five | 143/178 (80.3%) | 20/86 (23.3%) |
| No participant exclusion | 0.5 | 7/10 | All eight | 143/181 (79.0%) | 25/94 (26.6%) |
| Lower prediction cutoff | 0.4 | 7/10 | Same five | 185/250 (74.0%) | 45/161 (28.0%) |
| Higher prediction cutoff | 0.6 | 7/10 | Same five | 92/103 (89.3%) | 2/30 (6.7%) |
| Lower agreement cutoff | 0.5 | 6/10 | Same five | 157/219 (71.7%) | 6/45 (13.3%) |
| Higher agreement cutoff | 0.5 | 8/10 | Same five | 94/116 (81.0%) | 69/148 (46.6%) |

The original five participants are P1, P2, P3, P5, and P8. This cohort is held
fixed when varying either threshold; only the second row includes all eight
participants. Non-aligned voxels are well predicted but fall below the
direction-agreement cutoff in that row. Thus, at 8/10, the comparison group
also includes voxels that met the original 7/10 criterion.

The pooled LTPJ concentration is higher for aligned voxels under every
specification, although the size of the contrast varies across cutoffs.
These are descriptive sensitivity checks, not significance tests.
